The Infrastructure Bottleneck

Why Wired Fast Charging Cannot Scale in India

India's EV-to-public-charger ratio is 1:215. Plugging in creates idle fleet downtime, extreme grid spikes, and monsoon cable hazards.

Status Quo: Conductive Wired Plugs

Crippling Fleet Downtime: 45 minutes to 8 hours wasted per shift. Commercial cabs & logistics vans lose up to 20% of their revenue-earning hours.

Monsoon & Cable Wear Hazards: Handling heavy high-voltage cables in wet weather causes socket wear, corrosion, and electrical safety concerns.

Prohibitive Battery Weight: Cabs must carry massive 40-70 kWh battery packs (adding ₹2-4 Lakhs cost) just to fight range anxiety.

WireFree Charge Innovation

Zero Manual Effort: Automatic inductive connection upon parking (Phase A) or continuous opportunity charging while driving (Phase B).

Vehicle-Side Adaptive Intelligence: We place active auto-tuning circuits and suspension actuators on the vehicle. Road coils remain passive and indestructible.

Scheduled Repaving Integration: We don't dig up healthy roads; coils are installed during planned 5-7 year asphalt overlay or lane expansion tenders.
